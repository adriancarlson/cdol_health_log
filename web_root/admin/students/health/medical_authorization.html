Agreements

In the event of a severe asthma or allergic reaction ~(first_name)'s personal Action Plan will be followed. If ~(first_name) does not have an Action Plan, the Emergency Response to Life Threatening Asthma or the Systemic Allergic Reactions protocol will be followed.

School personnel may provide ~(first_name) with basic first aid care.

In the event of a serious injury or illness requiring emergency medical treatment, the first responders may determine which hospital to transport ~(first_name) for treatment. If the first responders do not determine which hospital, the school will attempt to contact parents, if possible, while coordinating with emergency responders to determine the most appropriate hospital for transport. If the school is unable to reach a parent, ~(first_name) will be transported either to the nearest hospital or a hospital chosen by the school.
